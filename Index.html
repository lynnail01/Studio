Welcome to The Nail & Lash Spot by Lyn

My name is Lyn. With over 9 years in the beauty industry, I specialize in hand-painted nail art, creating unique designs that reflect your personal style and bring your nail vision to life. Beyond nails, I also offer eyelash services to enhance your natural beauty and complete look. My goal is to create a welcoming experience where you feel appreciated, express your personal style, and leave with a smile.

With a passion for nail artistry and a commitment to affordable beauty, I want every client to feel that looking beautiful can be both special and accessible without compromising creativity or quality.
